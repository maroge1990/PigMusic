| 26 Oct 2026 | PLAY4 (de06009, 20afe9b), Codex round 2 | Medium | A stand-in is not looked for in an edition retired within the last 6 h with no lease, though `Mixes.entry(playback=True)` still plays such an edition. A rotated song whose only compatible copy is in an edition retired minutes ago therefore stays unavailable and is passed over. Fix: also select `retired_at > now - 6 h` in the candidate query (the final `playback()` check stays), with a regression for a candidate retired a minute ago. Reproduced by Codex in memory; not on a device | `server/mixes.py` `Mixes.stand_in` candidate query |
| 27 Oct 2026 | DIAG1 (667db9a, ef38339), Codex round 2 | Medium | The playback harness infers which song failed from acquisition failures and the last song heard, which `QueuePCMStream` does not fully expose. (a) If a song's transfer fails mid-song after the next song's preparation already failed, the harness blames the next song and hides the real failure. (b) A song whose decoder fails to open after a successful acquisition is passed over by the queue without the harness marking it failed; if every remaining decoder fails, the last healthy song is blamed. Diagnostic attribution only: the app's playback is unchanged. Fix: have `QueuePCMStream` report occurrence-indexed skips and the terminal failure's occurrence and cause, and use them in the harness, with real-queue tests for both cases. Found by reading `QueuePCMStream`; not reproduced | `swift/PigKit/Sources/PigPlaybackHarness/main.swift`, `PlaybackTimeline.failedSongs` |
| 27 Oct 2026 | DIAG3 (ec5418d, 73621ef), Codex round 2 | Medium | The diagnostics analyser's "Requests failed" finding counts only failures and 5xx statuses, so 401, 403 and 429 answers the phone logs (expired sign-in, denied playback, throttling) are not summarised as failures; they still appear in the slowest-requests list when slow. Fix: count statuses of 400 and above, with explicit exclusions only for expected outcomes, and add 401, 403 and 429 regressions. Reproduced by Codex in memory | `scripts/analyse_diagnostics.py` `findings` |
